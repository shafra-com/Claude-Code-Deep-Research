## 2. MARKET SIZE & GROWTH STATISTICS

### Claim 2.1: RWA Market Size 2024

**CLAIM:** "RWA tokenization market valued at approximately $300 billion in 2024"

**VERIFICATION STATUS:** ⚠️ **PARTIALLY VERIFIED - DATA VARIATION**

**Primary Source 1:**
- Next MSC Market Report: https://www.nextmsc.com/report/tokenized-real-world-assets-rwas-market-bf3345
- **Reported Value:** USD 297.71 billion (2024)
- Source Quality: **B** (Market research firm)
- Methodology: Top-down and bottom-up analysis, multilinear regression, ARIMA modeling

**Primary Source 2:**
- InvestaX Report: https://www.investax.io/blog/2024-real-world-asset-tokenization-market-recap
- **Reported Value:** $15.2 billion (excluding stablecoins) by December 2024
- Source Quality: **B** (Industry platform)

**Primary Source 3:**
- Various Industry Reports
- **Reported Value:** $217.26 billion (including stablecoins)
- Source Quality: **B-C**

**DISCREPANCY ANALYSIS:**
The significant variation in reported market size stems from **methodology differences**:
1. **Inclusion/Exclusion of Stablecoins:** Some reports include stablecoins ($140M+ accounts globally), others exclude them
2. **Asset Classification:** Different definitions of what constitutes a "tokenized RWA"
3. **Valuation Methods:** Some use AUM, others use market cap

**RECONCILIATION:**
- **Without Stablecoins:** $15.2B - $24B (verified across multiple sources)
- **With Stablecoins:** $217B - $297B (depends on stablecoin inclusion methodology)

**Confidence Rating:** **MEDIUM-HIGH (75%)** - Verified but with methodological variations

**RECOMMENDATION:** When citing market size, specify whether stablecoins are included or excluded.

## 7. CONTRADICTIONS & CLARIFICATIONS

### Contradiction 7.1: Market Size Definitions

**Issue:** Significant variance in reported RWA market size ($15.2B vs $297.71B)

**Explanation:**
- **Narrow Definition (excluding stablecoins):** $15.2B - $24B
- **Broad Definition (including stablecoins):** $217B - $297.71B
- **Stablecoin Market:** ~140M accounts, representing majority of tokenized dollar assets

**Resolution:** Always specify whether stablecoins are included in market size figures.

---

### Contradiction 7.2: Timeline Discrepancy

**Issue:** $30 trillion projection cited as both 2030 and 2034

**Clarification:**
- **Standard Chartered:** $30 trillion by **2034**
- **BCG:** $16 trillion by 2030
- **McKinsey:** $2-4 trillion by 2030

**Resolution:** The $30 trillion figure specifically refers to Standard Chartered's 2034 projection, not 2030.

---

### Contradiction 7.3: Plume Asset Figures

**Issue:** Multiple figures cited for Plume's tokenized assets ($1B, $1.25B, $4.5B, $150M)

**Clarification:**
- **$1B:** Allegiance mineral rights (specific partnership)
- **$1.25B:** Total announced tokenization pipeline
- **$4.5B:** Cumulative assets tokenized to date (lifetime)
- **$150M:** Initial mainnet launch deployment (January 2025)

**Resolution:** Figures represent different stages and scopes of tokenization activity.
