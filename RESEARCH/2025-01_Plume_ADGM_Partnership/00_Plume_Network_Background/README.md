# Plume Network: RWA Tokenization Platform Research

## Research Overview

This folder contains comprehensive research on Plume Network, a blockchain platform purpose-built for Real World Asset (RWA) tokenization and RWAfi (Real World Asset Finance).

**Research Date:** January 2025
**Focus Areas:** Technology architecture, RWA tokenization capabilities, ecosystem partnerships, regulatory compliance

## Key Findings Summary

- **Platform Type:** Layer 1 blockchain purpose-built for RWA tokenization
- **Launch Status:** Mainnet launched June 5, 2025
- **Assets Onboarded:** $4+ billion committed, $150M deployed at mainnet launch
- **Ecosystem Size:** 200+ projects building on Plume
- **Funding:** $30M+ raised (Series A: $20M, Seed: $10M)
- **Unique Features:** Arc tokenization engine, Proof of Representation consensus, SEC-registered transfer agent status
