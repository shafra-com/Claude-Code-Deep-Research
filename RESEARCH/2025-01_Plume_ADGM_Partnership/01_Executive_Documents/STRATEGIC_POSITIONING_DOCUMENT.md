## V. Regulatory Compliance & Licensing Strategy

### 5.1 ADGM FSRA Licensing Requirements

**Applicable License Categories for Plume Operations:**

#### License Type 1: Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF)
**Purpose:** Operate secondary market for tokenized asset trading
**Capital Requirement:** $2,000,000 USD (Category 2)
**Key Requirements:**
- Robust market conduct rules
- Operational substance in ADGM (staff, management, systems)
- AML/KYC procedures aligned with FATF standards
- Segregated client assets
- Market surveillance and manipulation prevention

**Precedent:** Kraken, Matrix Exchange, Yoshi Markets all hold MTF licenses in ADGM

#### License Type 2: Custodian (Providing Custody)
**Purpose:** Hold and safeguard tokenized assets on behalf of clients
**Capital Requirement:** $500,000 USD (Category 3)
**Key Requirements:**
- Segregated client asset accounts

**RWA (Real World Assets):** Physical or traditional financial assets tokenized on blockchain (real estate, bonds, commodities, etc.)

**Tokenization:** The process of representing ownership rights to assets as digital tokens on a blockchain

**ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Market):** International financial free zone in Abu Dhabi, UAE

**FSRA (Financial Services Regulatory Authority):** Independent regulator of financial services in ADGM

**MTF (Multilateral Trading Facility):** Regulated platform for trading financial instruments

**FRT (Fiat-Referenced Token):** Stablecoin backed by fiat currency reserves

**SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission):** United States federal securities regulator

**Transfer Agent:** Entity that maintains records of security holders and processes transfers

**EVM (Ethereum Virtual Machine):** Software environment for executing smart contracts

**DeFi (Decentralized Finance):** Financial applications built on blockchain without traditional intermediaries

**Arc:** Plume Network's open-source tokenization engine

**Proof of Representation (PoR):** Plume's consensus mechanism designed for real-world asset verification
